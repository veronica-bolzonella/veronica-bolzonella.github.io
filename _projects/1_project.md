In my Master's thesis project, supervised by Professors Tal Kachman (Radboud University) and Jason Bramburger (Concorida University), I explore the applicability of Neural ODEs and Neural SDEs to solve Graphon Mean Field Games. 


This project, which was awarded a Mitacs Globalink Award, is carried out in Radboud University (Nijmegen), and Concordia University (Montreal).

Joining diverse research teams and their respective expertise in Deep Learning and Mathematics, this project brings me a contious opportunity for growth. I learnt immensly not only in the study and application of these topics and methods, but also in the interaction with different researchers, pushing my ability to communicate science, and offering me a diverse range of scientific perspective.
