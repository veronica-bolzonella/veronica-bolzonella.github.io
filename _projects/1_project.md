In my Master's thesis project, supervised by Professors Tal Kachman (Radboud University) and Jason Bramburger (Concordia University), I explore the applicability of self-supervised Neural ODEs and Neural SDEs to solve Graphon Mean Field Games. 


This project, which was awarded a MITACS Globalink Award, is carried out at Radboud University (Nijmegen), and Concordia University (Montreal).

Joining diverse research teams and their respective expertise in Deep Learning and Mathematics, this project brings me a continous opportunity for growth. I learnt immensely not only in the study and application of these topics and methods, but also in the interaction with different researchers, pushing my ability to communicate science, and offering me a diverse range of scientific perspective.
