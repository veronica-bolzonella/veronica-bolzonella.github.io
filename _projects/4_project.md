In 2025 I participated in the Kaggle Competition Deep Past challenge together with a great team. Our task was to translate Old Akkadian text into English. 

With only a few clay tablets available, and ambiguous translations, data scarcity and quality was the real Deep Learning challenge. We finetuned two pre-trained models known to perform well in low-resource settings, mBART and T5. 

Alongside the finetuning, we developed augmentation strategies and tested different training techniques. 

This project was extremely fun, firstly as it was a highly academic and engaging problem, and secondly, because I was just too intrigued by all the gossip going on between the Akkadians in the old days: can you believe Assur-Sununu was caught stealing 5 shekels and still said in court he would do it again??
