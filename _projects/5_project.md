Me and my team participated in the BirdCLEF2026+ Kaggle Competition, an annual challenge that aims to automatically classify bird sounds from recordings of the wild.

We used a complex combination of Perch, SED Efficientnets, distillation and probing. This very intricate architecture taught us a lot about model development, and about many topics in machine hearing, deep learning, and data processing.
