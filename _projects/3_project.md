During my first year of Master, I conducted a research project under the supervision of Tal Kachman as a preparation to my extended research thesis.

In this project I developed a proof of concept method that uses Neural ODEs to solve a FOkker Plank Equation of language dynamics. In principle, this method can be used for language generation and developed into a small scale surrogate of Language Models.

This project thought me the fundamentals of research, from how to start and organize a research project, to how to tackle challenges and roadbumps. It is also in this project that I started building my knowledge in Neural ODEs, Jax and related frameworks, which became fundamental skills in my day to day research.
Looking into the ins and outs of Natural Language Processing, from Bigram models to LLMs, also thought me how complex and unpredictable language modelling really is! Making a language model from scratch and under a completely new perspective was no easy task, and put into perspective how the impressive the models that exists nowadays really are. The best thing my model told me? _"The blue blue sky is for the birds"_. And I was so proud!
