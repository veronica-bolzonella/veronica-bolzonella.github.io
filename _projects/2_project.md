In my Bachelor's thesis I experimented with the effect of different compression techniques, such as pruning and quantization, applied to Diffusion Model.

Supervised by Professor Luca Ambrogioni, I learnt a lot about how to approach research, formulate problems, and analyze solutions. Diffusion Models and the incredibly fascinating math behind them, became one of the main reasons I became interested in the fundamental themes in Deep Learning. 

My Thesis was concluded with an 8.5, and concluded my Bachelors journey, from which I graduated Cum Laude.
