In my Bachelor's thesis I experimented with the effect of different compression techniques, such as pruning and quantization, applied to Diffusion Models.

Supervised by Professor Luca Ambrogioni, I learnt a lot about how to approach research, formulate problems, and analyze solutions. Diffusion Models and the fascinating math behind them, became one of the main reasons I became interested in the mathematical formulation of Deep Learning.
