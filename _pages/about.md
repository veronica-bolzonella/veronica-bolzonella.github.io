I am Veronica, a Masters' student of Artificial Intellience fascienated by game theory, dynamical systems, and the world of Math in general. 

I started my Bachelors in AI at Radboud University, back in days when people would ask _"AI? What is that?"_. That was actually just a few years back, in 2022. Since then, I finished my Bachelors, started my Masters in Interactive Agents, and peple stopped asking me what AI is and started telling me what they think about LLMs. 
In the years I spent learning, I found and shaped my niche within the vast field of AI in the intersection of Deep Learning and Applied Mathematics. 
My spark lays in the most fundamental mathematical foundations of AI.
Simultaneously, through inspiring professors around me and personal exploration, I developed a keen interest for Dynamical Systems and Game Theory. 
A combination of these elements brought me to my Masters research project. In this project I explore the use of Neural Differential Equations to solve complex heterogeneous systems in the framework of Graphon Mean Field Games. 
This research not only expanded my academic horizon to new fields and topics, but also allowed me to connect to researchers around the globe, who I admire and inspire me to continue my journey in academic research.
My goal is to contribute to the academic community in fundamental Deep Learning and Mathematics, creating solutions that can help the world find its Equilibrium. I hope to make a career out of my passion for research, and never cease to be curious and inquisitive. 

Beyond the academic sphere, I enjoy bouldering, running, and raising giant monsteras or weirdly shaped bamboos. 
Growing up in Italy, food is my love language, my comfort, and a form of creative expression and discovery. I am in a forever quest to match my parents' pizza recipe, and to master the perfect vegan home made ice cream, a research that I approach with scientific precision and religious commitment. I am happy to share the forever evolving results with any food enthusiast out there!

On a professional level, I also nourish my passion for Math, as I am a tutor specialized in IB Math AA and AI, and I have been tutoring and teaching revision courses with IB Academy since 2022. Alongside teaching, I co-authored the (https://ib-academy.nl/sgs)[IB Academy Math Study Guides]. At IB Academy I also cover the roles of Project Manager and Recruiter.
