I am Veronica, a Masters' student of Artificial Intellience fascienated by game theory, dynamical systems, and the world of Math and theoretical AI in general. 

I started my Bachelors in AI at Radboud University, back in the days when people would ask _"AI? What is that?"_. That was actually just a few years back, in 2022. Since then, I finished my Bachelors, started my Masters in Interactive Agents, and peple stopped asking me what AI is and started telling me what they think about LLMs. 
In the years I spent learning, I found and shaped my niche within the vast field of AI in the intersection of Deep Learning and Applied Mathematics. 
My spark lays in the most fundamental mathematical foundations of AI.
Simultaneously, through inspiring professors around me and personal exploration, I developed a keen interest for Dynamical Systems and Game Theory. 
My Masters thesis _Neural GMFGs_ is an example of a project that grows out of this combination of elements. In this project I explore the use of Neural Differential Equations to solve complex heterogeneous systems in the framework of Graphon Mean Field Games. 
This research not only expanded my academic horizon to new fields and topics, but also allowed me to connect to researchers around the globe, who I admire and inspire me to continue my journey in academic research.
My goal is to contribute to the academic community in fundamental Deep Learning and Mathematics, creating solutions that can help the world find its Equilibrium. 
My goal is to never cease to find the beauty and fulfillment one feels when solving a math problem. As Grant Sanderson beautifuly puts it:

> There is a joy simply to doing math. A very specific emotion, it's like a clicking in your head. If I'm engaging  with a good problem and I see the way it's going to be solved or if I see how a new theory fits together, it gives me a completely different way of thinking. there are a lot of different parts of math that give that feeling of reshaping your thoughts. (...). It's just satisfying. It's just nice.
> —Grant Sanderson on ["There's never been a better time to go into math"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKrwhjOI8Nc)


Beyond the academic sphere, I enjoy bouldering, running, and raising giant monsteras. I am also passionate about cooking. Growing up in Italy, food became my love language, my comfort, and a form of creative expression and discovery. I am in a forever quest to match my parents' pizza recipe, and to master the perfect vegan home made ice cream, a research that I approach with scientific precision and religious commitment. I am happy to share the forever evolving results with any food enthusiast out there!

On a professional level, I also nourish my passion for Math as a tutor specialized in IB Math AA and AI. I have been tutoring and teaching revision courses with IB Academy since 2022. Alongside teaching, I co-authored the [IB Academy Math Study Guides](https://ib-academy.nl/sgs). At IB Academy I also cover the roles of Project Manager and Recruiter.
